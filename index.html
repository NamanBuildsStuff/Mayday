Reminder: You Are Endangering Yourself

Approaching an active emergency puts your own life and health at serious risk. Professional responders are trained and equipped for these hazards — you are not.

  • Never enter burning buildings, smoke-filled rooms, flood currents, or unstable structures.
  • Only provide minimal safe-distance help (clearing access roads for fire trucks, guiding ambulances, warning pedestrians, or offering water/first aid away from the hazard).
  • Leave immediately if smoke, heat, structural creaking, or water levels worsen.
